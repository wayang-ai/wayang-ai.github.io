# About Wayang AI

## Our Mission

Wayang AI is building the future of intelligent automation. We believe that AI agents should work together seamlessly, just like a well-orchestrated team, to solve complex problems and automate workflows.

---

## What is Wayang?

"Wayang" comes from the traditional Indonesian puppet theater, where a master puppeteer (dalang) orchestrates multiple characters to tell compelling stories. Similarly, our platform enables you to orchestrate multiple AI agents to accomplish complex tasks.

### Wayang Assistant

Wayang includes an internal assistant that helps users build projects, answer questions about Wayang, and troubleshoot errors using official documentation. See [Wayang Assistant docs](/docs/wayang-assistant).

### The Vision

We envision a world where:
- **AI agents collaborate** seamlessly to achieve complex goals
- **Developers focus on creativity** rather than infrastructure
- **Automation is accessible** to everyone, not just AI experts
- **Intelligent workflows** adapt and learn from experience

## Technology Stack

Wayang AI is built with modern, battle-tested technologies:

| Layer | Technology |
|-------|------------|
| **Runtime** | Node.js, TypeScript |
| **Workflow Engine** | Custom event-driven architecture |
| **AI Integration** | LangChain, LlamaIndex |
| **Storage** | PostgreSQL, Redis |
| **API** | REST, GraphQL |
| **UI** | React, TypeScript |
| **Deployment** | Docker, Kubernetes |
