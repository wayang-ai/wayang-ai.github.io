# Cookies Policy

**Last Updated:** March 21, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

This Cookies Policy explains how Wayang AI ("we," "us," "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and documentation platform (collectively, the "Site"). By using our Site, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

---

## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They help website owners provide functionality, understand how their site is used, and enhance user experience.

### Types of Cookies We Use:

| Type | Purpose | Duration |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Essential Cookies** | Required for basic site functionality | Session |
| **Performance Cookies** | Help us understand site usage and performance | Session/Persistent |
| **Functionality Cookies** | Remember your preferences and settings | Persistent |
| **Analytics Cookies** | Collect anonymous usage statistics | Persistent |

---

## 3. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

### 3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the Site to function properly:

- **Session Management**: Maintain your session while browsing documentation
- **Security**: Protect against cross-site request forgery (CSRF) attacks
- **Load Balancing**: Distribute traffic across servers for optimal performance

**Legal Basis**: Legitimate interest (necessary for site operation)

### 3.2 Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our Site:

- **Page Views**: Track which documentation pages are most visited
- **Error Monitoring**: Identify technical errors and broken links
- **Performance Metrics**: Measure page load times and server response
- **Search Analytics**: Understand what users search for on our Site

**Data Collected**:
- Pages visited
- Time spent on pages
- Referral source
- Device type and browser information
- IP address (anonymized)

**Legal Basis**: Your consent (where required by applicable law)

### 3.3 Functionality Cookies

These cookies remember your choices and preferences:

- **Language Preference**: Remember your selected language
- **Theme Settings**: Remember dark/light mode preferences
- **Search History**: Store recent searches for your convenience
- **Collapsed/Expanded Sections**: Remember navigation state

**Legal Basis**: Your consent

## 5. Cookie Consent

### 5.1 Consent Management

When you first visit our Site, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner. You can:

- **Accept All**: Consent to all cookie categories
- **Reject Non-Essential**: Only allow essential cookies
- **Customize**: Choose which cookie categories to allow
- **Withdraw Consent**: Change your preferences at any time

### 5.2 How to Manage Cookies

**Browser Settings**:
Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:
- View cookies stored on your device
- Delete individual cookies or all cookies
- Block third-party cookies
- Block cookies from specific sites
- Clear cookies when you close your browser

**Browser-Specific Instructions**:
- [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enhanced-tracking-protection-firefox-desktop)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
- [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)

**Mobile Devices**:
- [iOS Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT201265)
- [Android Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid)
